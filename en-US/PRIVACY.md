# Snow Globe · Privacy policy

[Support](SUPPORT.md) · [Privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) · [Languages](../README.md)

Snow Globe uses the system photo picker to access the image you choose. The iPhone analyzes depth and creates the scene on-device. If the original is in iCloud Photos, Apple may download it when you select it. You can send the resulting scene to your paired Apple Watch using Apple WatchConnectivity.

Wrist motion is used on the watch to move the scene and stir particles. The app does not keep a motion history. Snow Globe has no account system, developer-operated photo server, advertising or analytics SDK.

The app keeps a draft and the most recent transferred scene on your devices. Remove the draft in the iPhone studio. To remove all app-held copies, uninstall Snow Globe on both devices. Deleting the app leaves your originals in Photos. You can stop choosing photos or sending scenes at any time and manage any motion permission shown in system settings.

If you email support, the address and information you send are used to handle your request. Email the same address to request access or deletion of that correspondence. GitHub hosts this page and handles visits under its own privacy statement.

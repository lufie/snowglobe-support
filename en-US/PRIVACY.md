# Snow Globe · Privacy policy

[Support](SUPPORT.md) · [Privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) · [Languages](../README.md)

Snow Globe uses only the photo you choose. Your iPhone works out the depth and fills in the background. We don’t upload your photo to our servers. The finished scene goes to your paired Apple Watch only when you tap Send to Watch. If the original is in iCloud Photos, Apple may download it when you select it.

Wrist motion is used on the watch to move the scene and stir particles. The app does not keep a motion history. Snow Globe has no account system, developer-operated photo server, advertising or analytics SDK.

You can delete saved scenes on your iPhone. The original stays in Photos. A scene you’ve already sent stays on your watch until you replace it by sending another one. To remove the copies kept by Snow Globe on both devices, delete the app from each device.

When you import music, your iPhone processes the selected audio and tries to send a playable copy to your paired Apple Watch if Snow Globe is installed there. Apple handles purchases and renewals.

If you email support, the address and information you send are used to handle your request. Email the same address to request access or deletion of that correspondence. GitHub hosts this page and handles visits under its own privacy statement.
